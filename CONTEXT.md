# Portfolio

The Portfolio is the owner's public professional presence. It presents the owner's work and professional information and gives visitors a way to make contact.

## Portfolio content

**Project**
A piece of work presented in the Portfolio. A Project includes its professional context, case-study content, Technologies, Categories, links, publication state, and Media assets.
_Avoid_: Work, case study, portfolio item when referring to the complete concept.

**Case study**
The substantive presentation of a Project, explaining the project through structured content such as its overview, problem, process, solution, and results.
_Avoid_: Project when referring specifically to the explanatory content.

**Technology**
A technology, tool, or technical capability associated with a Project.
_Avoid_: Stack, tag, tool when referring to the canonical concept.

**Category**
A professional classification describing the kind of work represented by a Project.
_Avoid_: Tag when the classification has a defined professional meaning.

**Media asset**
A visual or audiovisual asset associated with a Project, such as an image, gallery item, or video. A Media asset has presentation metadata and belongs to a Project.
_Avoid_: File, attachment when referring to the asset as a portfolio domain concept.

## Professional contact

**Inquiry**
A request submitted by a visitor who wants to contact the Portfolio owner, usually about potential work.
_Avoid_: Contact, message, lead when referring to the request as a portfolio domain concept.

**Client**
A person or organization that has an established professional relationship with the Portfolio owner. An Inquiry does not by itself make someone a Client.
_Avoid_: Lead when referring to an established relationship.

**Availability**
The Portfolio owner's current stated availability for professional work.
_Avoid_: Status when referring specifically to whether the owner is available for work.

## Content lifecycle

**Draft**
A Project that is being prepared and is not part of the public Portfolio.

**Published**
A Project that is intentionally available as part of the public Portfolio.

**Archived**
A Project that is no longer part of the normal public Portfolio but is retained as part of the Portfolio's history.

## Management

**User**
A person who can access private Portfolio management functionality.
_Avoid_: Admin when referring to the person itself rather than their role or access level.

**Portfolio owner**
The person whose professional work and information the Portfolio represents. The first production system is optimized for one practical owner, but the domain does not assume only one User exists.
_Avoid_: Admin when referring to the owner as a domain concept.
